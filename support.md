# StableLog support

StableLog keeps a horse's records, care log and paperwork on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account and nothing is stored on our side, which also means **we cannot recover records for you. Your backup file is your safety net.**

Need help that is not answered here? Write to [hello@insparq.app](mailto:hello@insparq.app).

## Back up your records

1. Open **Settings** (the last tab) and find **Backup and export**.
2. Tap **Export a backup**.
3. Choose where to save it: Files, iCloud Drive or any other place you trust.

A backup is one .zip file with every record, photo and document, plus your reminder settings and your name and phone number if you entered them. "Last backup" in Settings shows when you last made one. Backups are free and always will be.

It is worth making one after any big batch of entries, and before you change phones.

**One horse only:** open the horse, tap **Reports and more** (the "…" button), then **Export this horse…**. This makes a backup file with just that horse, for a buyer or the end of a lease. The other person imports it like any backup.

## Restore from a backup

1. Open **Settings → Backup and export → Import a backup**.
2. Pick the .zip file.

Importing adds the backup to what is already in the app. If some of the records in the backup are already on the device, StableLog tells you how many would be replaced and when the backup was made, and waits for you to choose **Import and replace** or **Cancel**. Replacing puts those records back to how they were in the backup, so changes made to them since are lost. Records that are not in the backup are left alone.

After a restore:

- If notifications were on, the app asks to turn them on again.
- The app lock is not restored. Turn it on again in Settings if you use it.
- StableLog Pro is not part of a backup. See "Restore purchases" below.

## Moving to a new phone

1. On the old phone, export a backup and save it somewhere the new phone can reach, such as iCloud Drive.
2. Install StableLog on the new phone.
3. Import the backup (Settings → Backup and export → Import a backup).
4. If you bought Pro, tap **Restore purchases** in Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Apple's own phone-to-phone transfer and iCloud Backup normally carry an app's data across as well, but make a StableLog backup file first so you are not relying on it.

StableLog does not sync between devices. Each device has its own copy of the records.

## StableLog Pro

The free version does everything for one current horse, with no limit on entries, documents or reminders. Pro adds more than one current horse, adding to horses you have sold or lost, PDFs without a watermark, custom reports, the expense summary, the feeding card, the Summary view, the spending breakdown and spreadsheet (CSV) export.

Pro is sold three ways that unlock the same thing: monthly, yearly, or once (lifetime).

### How to cancel a subscription

- On your device: **Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → StableLog**, then cancel.
- Or in StableLog: **Settings → Manage or cancel subscription**.

Cancel at least a day before the renewal date. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. The lifetime purchase has nothing to cancel.

Billing and refunds are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### What happens when a subscription ends

StableLog goes back to the free version. **Nothing is deleted.**

- If you have more than one current horse, the horse you used most recently stays open for now, and the app asks whether to keep it or choose another. Either way it asks you to confirm, because you choose once: changing it later needs Pro.
- The other current horses are shown under "Locked" on the Horses list. Their records are safe and are still included in your backups, but they cannot be opened, they send no notifications and they are left out of totals. Tapping one offers its complete record as a PDF.
- Sold and deceased horses can still be looked at, shared and exported, but nothing can be added to them.
- PDFs carry a watermark again, and the Pro reports, Summary, spending breakdown and CSV export are locked.

Subscribe again, or buy the lifetime option, and everything opens exactly as you left it.

### Restore purchases

If you bought Pro and the app shows the free version (a new phone, or after reinstalling), open **Settings → Restore purchases**. The same button is on the Pro screen. You must be signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

## Notifications are not arriving

- Check notifications are switched on in StableLog's **Settings**.
- Check notifications are allowed for StableLog in the iOS Settings app.
- Notifications come from the dates and intervals you entered. A care item with no interval, or a document with no expiry date, has nothing to send a notification about.
- A phone keeps a limited number of notifications waiting at once. StableLog schedules the soonest ones first and sets the rest the next time you open the app. If you have many horses or long courses of medication, open the app from time to time. A medication's form tells you how far its dose notifications are set.

## Scanned papers

When you attach a scan, photo or PDF, StableLog reads it on your device and may suggest a type, dates, a total or a provider. These are suggestions. Each one shows the words it was read from and is used only if you tap it, and nothing is filed until you tap Save. Always check a suggestion against the paper: it can be wrong, especially on handwriting, faint print or an unusual layout.

Password-protected PDFs cannot be stored; the app says so when you choose one.

## Known limits

- **One person, one device.** Two people cannot share one set of records, and there is no sync between devices. To hand records to someone else, send a backup or a single-horse export.
- **Sold or deceased is one-way in the free version.** The app asks you to confirm first. Pro can change a horse back.
- **A locked horse cannot be opened or deleted in the free version.** It is kept until you unlock Pro or erase all data. You can still get everything on file for it: tap the horse under "Locked" and choose "Make its Horse Record (PDF)". The PDF has its details, every entry with its cost, what is coming due, and its papers and photos, and can be saved or shared like any other.
- **Importing an older backup replaces newer edits** to the same records, after asking you. The app cannot keep "whichever is newer".
- **A paper that arrives by email** can be opened straight into StableLog: touch and hold the attachment in Mail (or tap Share) and choose StableLog.
- **A receipt for a bill split across several horses** is filed as a document under one horse. The entries on the other horses still carry its photo.
- **Currency.** A cost is entered in your device's currency and keeps it; totals in more than one currency say so.
- **English only** for now.
- **iPad** uses the same layout as iPhone.
- **Very large backups** (over 4 GB) cannot be written.
- StableLog keeps records. It does not give veterinary, medical or legal advice, it suggests no schedules, and its reports are your own records, not certificates.

## Erase everything

**Settings → Erase all data** removes every horse, record, photo and document. It cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you might want the records later. Deleting the app from your device removes all of its data too.
