# StableLog privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

StableLog is made by TFT Ventures LLC, trading as InSparq. This page says what the app does with what you put into it.

## The short version

- Everything you enter stays on your iPhone or iPad.
- We collect nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- We run no server for StableLog, so there is nowhere for your records to be sent.
- Your records leave your device only when you export or share something yourself.
- If you write to us, we see only what you choose to send.

## What the app stores, and where

StableLog stores what you type and attach: your horses, care entries, costs, feeding plans, the vets, farriers and other people you add, photos, scanned papers and PDFs, your reminder settings, and your own name and phone number if you enter them in Settings.

All of it is kept in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it.

If you open a PDF or a photo from Mail or Files in StableLog, the app receives a copy of that file. It is kept only if you save it as a document; if you cancel, the copy is removed.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your records, your photos, your contacts, your location, a device identifier or usage statistics to us or to anyone else. There is no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

## Reading scanned papers

When you attach a scan, photo or PDF, StableLog can read it and suggest a document type, dates, a total or a provider. The reading happens on your device, using Apple's built-in text recognition and, on devices that have one, the device's own language model. The paper and its text are not sent anywhere. The words that were read are stored with the document on your device so that search can find it.

## Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only when you take a photo or scan a paper. Photos you pick from your library are chosen through Apple's photo picker, so the app sees only the ones you select. Photos and scans are stored inside the app.

If you choose to save a photo or a paper from StableLog to your Photos library, iOS asks you once whether StableLog may add to Photos. That permission lets the app add pictures only; it cannot see or read your library.

## Notifications

Notifications are scheduled on your device from the dates and intervals you enter. They are not sent from a server, and you can switch them off in StableLog's Settings or in iOS Settings.

## App lock

If you turn on "Lock StableLog", your device checks Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when the app is opened. Apple's system does the check and tells the app only whether it passed. StableLog never sees or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode.

## Purchases

StableLog Pro is bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details, and the app does not ask you for a name or email address to buy.

## Backups, reports and anything you share

A backup is a single .zip file you export from Settings and save where you choose. It holds your records, photos and documents, your reminder settings, and your name and phone number if you entered them. PDF reports and spreadsheet (CSV) files are also made only when you ask.

Once you save or send one of these files, it is in your hands and in the hands of whoever you give it to. Treat a backup like the paperwork it contains.

## Safety copies

Just before "Import and replace" or "Erase all data", StableLog writes a safety copy: a full backup of everything that was in the app, photos included, so that it can be brought back. Safety copies stay on your device, inside the app. They are not sent anywhere, and they are left out of your device's iCloud or computer backup. StableLog keeps the newest three, and also the one that holds the most records. You can share or delete each one under Settings › Safety copies.

## Writing to us

Settings › Contact support shows our address and can open Mail with it filled in. Nothing is sent unless you write and send the email yourself, from your own Mail app. We then receive what you wrote and your email address, and use them only to answer you.

Settings › Contact support › Report a problem shows a short report you can add to your email or share. You see all of it before it goes anywhere, and it is sent only if you send it. It holds counts, codes and a few settings only: the app's version and build, the iOS version, the device model (not its name), your device's time zone, calendar, region and text size, whether Pro is on, how many horses, entries, reminders, papers, contacts, feed items, stored files and safety copies there are, the storage they take, whether notifications are allowed and how many are set, and the code of the last error saving, if any. It holds no names, notes, dates or anything else you typed.

## Other apps your device may open

If you tap a phone number, an email address or an address in StableLog, your device opens Phone, Mail or Maps. If you tap the link to published vaccination guidance, the support page, the privacy policy or the Terms of Use, your device opens the web browser. Those apps and websites have their own privacy terms.

## Your device's own backup

If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Apple's backup includes StableLog's records, photos and documents along with the rest of your device. Safety copies are not included. That backup is between you and Apple; we have no access to it.

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage figures for the app. They do not contain your records.

## Deleting your data

- **Erase the records:** Settings → Erase all data removes every horse, record, reminder, contact, feed item, photo and document from the app, and your name and phone number in Settings → Owner. It leaves your preferences, such as units, appearance, notification times and the app lock.
- **The safety copy made before the erase stays on the device,** with any earlier ones, so the records can be brought back. To leave nothing behind, delete them under Settings › Safety copies.
- **Remove everything:** deleting StableLog from your device removes all of its data from that device, safety copies included.
- Backup files, PDFs and spreadsheets you saved or sent elsewhere are not touched by any of these steps. Delete those where you put them.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have written to us, we can delete your emails on request.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this page, we will update it and change the date at the top.
